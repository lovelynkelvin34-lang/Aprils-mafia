# 🔪 April's Mafia

Welcome to April's Mafia — a private online Mafia game for friends.

## How to play

1. Create a private room.
2. Share the room code with your friends.
3. Everyone joins from their own phone.
4. Roles are assigned secretly.
5. Mafia eliminate players at night.
6. Police investigate.
7. Doctor protects a player.
8. Everyone discusses and votes during the day.
9. Eliminated players enter the Afterlife.
10. The game ends when either the Mafia or Citizens win.

## Roles

- 🔪 Mafia
- 👮 Police
- 🩺 Doctor
- 👥 Citizens

Have fun and trust nobody. 😈
